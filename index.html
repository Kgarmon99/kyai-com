What happens next

From your submission to the public pulse.

Every lead goes through the same open review path before it shows up on the site.

  1. Submitted Your idea, lead, profile, or toolkit request arrives through the GitHub issue forms.
  2. Needs review Editors check the source link, date, region, category, people, and relevance.
  3. Verified The signal has enough source backing to enter the next public build.
  4. Published The signal, profile, event, toolkit, or mission is live and shareable.
