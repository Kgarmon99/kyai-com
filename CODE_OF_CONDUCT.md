# Code of Conduct

KYAI is a civic knowledge project. Keep contributions useful, sourced, respectful, and focused on helping Kentucky understand and use AI well.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still checking facts.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Avoid harassment, spam, private personal data, and bad-faith arguments.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these standards.
