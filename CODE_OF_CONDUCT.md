# Code of Conduct

KYAI is a civic knowledge project. Keep contributions useful, sourced,
respectful, and focused on helping Kentucky understand and use AI well.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still checking facts.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Avoid harassment, spam, private personal data, and bad-faith arguments.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
  or at KYAI events and workshops.
- Publishing someone's private information, including a person's home address,
  personal phone number, or private correspondence.
- Submitting knowingly false or unsourced claims about a person, school,
  business, or public official.
- Using KYAI's contributor channels to promote a product, campaign, or
  candidate rather than to inform.
- Sustained disruption of review discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every KYAI space: this repository, its issues and pull
requests, the public site's community surfaces, and KYAI workshops and events,
online or in person. It also applies when someone is representing KYAI
publicly.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers:

- Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Kgarmon99/kyai-com/security/advisories)
  for anything that involves exposed personal data.
- For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer listed in
  [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) directly through GitHub.

Reports are handled confidentially. Tell us what happened, where, and whether
you want to be contacted about the outcome. You will not be penalized for
reporting in good faith, and you do not have to interact with the person
involved.

If a report concerns a maintainer, you may send it to any other maintainer
listed in CODEOWNERS.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these
standards. Depending on severity and whether the behavior repeats, maintainers
may:

1. Give a private warning explaining what was wrong.
2. Ask for a public correction, where the harm was public.
3. Temporarily block someone from the repository or from KYAI events.
4. Permanently block someone.

Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith may be removed from the
maintainer list.
