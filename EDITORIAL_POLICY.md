# KYAI Editorial Policy

KYAI tracks AI signals that matter to Kentucky: education, workforce, research, public policy, civic use, infrastructure, events, organizations, and practical community resources.

## Publishing Standard

A signal can publish when it has:

- A working source URL
- A clear Kentucky connection
- A date or time window
- A region
- A category
- A short "why it matters"
- Enough context that a reader can act, learn, share, or verify

## Statuses

- `Submitted`: received from the community.
- `Needs review`: waiting for source or editor review.
- `Verified`: source-backed and ready for a build.
- `Published`: live on KYAI.
- `Archived`: outdated, superseded, or no longer relevant.

## Corrections

Open a correction issue with the exact claim, the requested change, and a source. Editors should correct material errors quickly and leave a note in the issue.

## Moderation

KYAI does not publish private personal data, unsourced accusations, confidential documents, spam, harassment, or partisan attack content. Local relevance matters more than volume.
